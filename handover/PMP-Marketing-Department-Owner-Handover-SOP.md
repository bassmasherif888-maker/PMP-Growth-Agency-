# PMP MARKETING DEPARTMENT
MODE OF OPERATIONS

Owner handover SOP | Version 1.0 | 30 October 2026

Purpose: transfer the department’s operating blueprint from the Marketing Manager to the agency owner—how work is acquired, prepared, assigned, reviewed, approved, published and improved.

Status: prepared from user-confirmed operating rules and supplied reference documents. It is not a claim that owner acceptance, access transfer, contract verification, outstanding-task closure or live-system testing is complete.

## 1. How to use this handover

Use the operating map to understand the whole department; use SOP-01–12 to execute a stage; use the role/tool tables for daily coordination. The accompanying PowerPoint is the owner briefing, not the live task register. Client names, employee names, prices, credentials and actual performance records are intentionally omitted from this shareable pack.

| Control | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed operating rule | Directly supplied by the user, including AM workflow, video reporting, submission rules, acquisition and payment gates. |
| Supplied framework | Growth OS six stages and SSM Strategy method. A framework is not proof that a client deliverable or audit was completed. |
| Proposed / owner decision | Routines, thresholds or authority not explicitly confirmed. They must not be presented as current company policy. |
| Acceptance / successor | Agency owner is the recipient. Confirm whether the current Marketing Manager retains approval authority; name a successor/delegate only if responsibility changes. Receipt alone does not change that authority. |

## 2. End-to-end operating map

Sales/BD prospecting → audit + initial proposal + three-tier quotation → first meeting and actual brief → CLIENT READY TO SIGN → AM-led actual strategy with SM/MM/MB → second meeting and four signed materials → move client folder → required upfront payment → AM production brief and task distribution → execution → quality review → Marketing Manager approval → AM collects one PPTX → final client approval → final archive + Social Media scheduling/publication → reporting and next-cycle actions.

| Milestone | What it permits | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client ready to sign | Start AM-led actual-strategy development after meeting one. | Contract signed; payment received; production authorized. |
| Contract signed | Move the existing folder out of main Quotations to a standalone client folder. | Contracted production may start before required payment. |
| Signed pack + verified upfront payment | Start contracted delivery within agreed scope. | Publication permission or automatic approval of later assets. |
| Head quality + Marketing approval | AM may collect finalized work into the client PPTX. | Client has approved it. |
| Final client approval of that version | Hand approved presentation/calendar to SM; archive final Design/Video copies. | Approval covers subsequent changed versions. |

## SOP-01 | Prospect and qualify

| Field | Instruction |
| --- | --- |
| Lead / participants | Sales OR Business Developer; Account Manager involved throughout. |
| Trigger | A potentially suitable business or an inbound lead is identified. |
| Required inputs | Social-channel review, contact details and a relevant service need; lead source. |

### Execution steps

1. Use on-ground visits, researched cold calls, LinkedIn search, email campaigns or calls to leads generated by PMP media buying.

2. Record the opportunity, contact, need, outreach outcome and next action in the agreed lead register. The lead-register tool and fields are proposed pending owner confirmation.

3. Book an online or offline first meeting. Keep the Account Manager involved.

4. Prepare a prospect/client-named folder inside PMP Drive / Quotations. Sales or BD is accountable for folder setup.

### Output and exit gate

Qualified opportunity, first meeting booked and pre-contract folder.

Gate: Do not confuse a booked meeting with a signed client or permission to start contracted delivery.

Required record: Lead/contact record and meeting details; prospect folder reference.

## SOP-02 | Prepare the audit and initial proposal

| Field | Instruction |
| --- | --- |
| Lead / participants | Sales/BD prepares and studies the meeting material; Account Manager involved. |
| Trigger | First meeting booked. |
| Required inputs | Available business brief, social URLs, website, asset access and current/historical pricing evidence. |

### Execution steps

1. Use supplied SKILL-1.md (SSM Strategy): confirm scope, format, language and available access.

2. Research before prescribing: brief, social channels, website, assets, competitors and category context. Record dated sources and what cannot be verified.

3. Label Observed facts, Interpretation and Recommendation separately. Public views alone do not prove paid boosting.

### Output and exit gate

Evidence-based audit, initial proposal strategy and three suggested quotation tiers.

Gate: The strategy skill supplies a research method, not an agency rate card or quotation formula. No invented prices or audit facts.

Required record: Audit/proposal deck, research notes, source dates, pricing references and declared gaps.

Gate: No publication before final client approval. Keep the approval tied to the actual presented version; do not reuse approval for changed material.

## 8. Task and approval-state rules

Track work stage separately from head approval, Marketing approval and external client approval. Recommended canonical work stages: Not started, In progress, Review pending, Revision required, Internally finalized, Awaiting client approval, Client approved, Scheduled, Published, Completed, Blocked/on hold, Cancelled. These normalized names are a proposal; do not rewrite historical workbook meaning without a mapping decision.

Minimum recommended task record: unique task ID; account/project; task/brief; accountable holder; priority; due/start/end dates; dependencies/inputs; deliverable link; relevant reviewer; quality decision; Marketing decision; presentation/version reference; client decision; comments with author/date; owner response and review outcome; final archive/live link. Additional columns are proposed controls, not claims about existing workbook structure.

## 10. Growth OS and evidence method

The supplied Growth OS provides the strategic loop: Discover → Diagnose → Design → Deploy → Optimise → Scale. Pre-sales research supplies early discovery evidence; the actual strategy reconciles it with the real brief at readiness to sign. Contracted execution then follows the signed scope and payment gate. Reporting feeds future diagnosis/design rather than bypassing approval.

SSM Strategy: confirm scope/format/language/access; research; identify contradictions; label Observed / Interpretation / Recommendation; disclose gaps; develop scoped strategy; verify numbers/quotes and presentation. A full scope contains 26 sections; audit-only work does not imply the full strategy is completed. Three quotation tiers require separately verified market ranges and previous prices.

## 10A. Monthly team performance appraisals

CONFIRMED cadence: complete team performance appraisals every month using the approved role-specific company forms. Exact dates, reviewer assignments, master file/version and private storage/access must be agreed. This is a people-development review, separate from client performance reporting and daily task approval.

1. Select the approved role-specific form and record employee, actual review month and confirmed direct reviewer privately. Do not infer a reviewer from coordination responsibilities.

2. Collect month-specific evidence from tasks, quality/revision records, outputs and role-appropriate results. Distinguish missing inputs or changed scope from individual execution.

3. Employee completes Self-Rating before the review meeting. Manager completes Manager Rating; compare the gap rather than assuming agreement.

4. Use the supplied 1–5 scale and behavioral anchors. Ratings 1, 2 and 5 require a brief evidence-based justification in the Notes field/section.

5. Check the role-specific weights and calculations. Supplied Excel uses Gap = Manager minus Self and weighted contribution = KPI weight × Manager Rating / 5. Confirm completion before interpreting totals: blank manager ratings are treated as zero in the supplied formulas.

6. Hold the monthly review conversation, record achievements, blockers, strengths, improvement areas and employee feedback. Track the template's quarterly OKRs within the monthly conversation; do not confuse the two cadences.

7. Agree SMART goals and development/training actions for the next period, with accountable action owners and agreed follow-up. Record the signatures/decisions required by the adopted process.

8. Keep completed appraisals and salary/career information in private authorized storage; carry goals into the following monthly review. Promotion, salary, succession and HR fields are not automatic decisions or approvals.

| Section | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Core competencies | 30% |
| Role-specific or technical KPIs | 50% |
| Professional development | 10% |
| Company events and culture | 10% |

Source scale: 1 Poor; 2 Below Expectations; 3 Meets Expectations; 4 Exceeds Expectations; 5 Outstanding. Use supplied behavioral anchors and evidence; justify ratings 1, 2 and 5. Blank ratings must not be read as a valid completed assessment.

The supplied templates include quarterly OKRs; track them during monthly reviews rather than changing monthly appraisal cadence. Final pay, promotion and HR decisions require authorized review; they are not automatically granted by a score. No completed employee evaluations, salaries or private archive files are included in this public pack.

Open decisions: Approved master/version; mapping nine supplied role-title families to the 14 positions (including missing/combined-role cases); reviewer of Marketing Manager; final HR/owner authority; exact deadlines and private storage/access. Do not create extra staff positions or force mismatched scorecards.
