What is confirmed, and what still needs a decision?

Confirmed user rules are distinct from suggested routines. Asset issue/return logs, receiver verification and card-clearing/backup safeguards are recommended controls to adopt. Exact SLAs, authority limits, hard-drive custody, software seats and training sign-off remain to be confirmed. No actual client/staff records, credentials, prices or performance data are included.
